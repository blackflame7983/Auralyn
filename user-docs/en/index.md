## What Auralyn does {#overview}

Auralyn processes microphone audio with noise reduction, voice adjustments and external VST effects. Set up and listen before a stream or call.

## What you need {#requirements}

Prepare a Windows PC, microphone and listening device. A virtual cable may be needed to send audio to OBS or a calling app. For hardware loopback, check your device's support and settings.

## Getting started {#start}

1. Choose a purpose, microphone and output in [First setup](./setup.md#purpose).
2. Make small changes in [Adjusting sound](./sound.md#noise-reduction), then listen.
3. Confirm reception in [OBS](./obs.md#connect) or your [calling app](./discord.md#connect).
4. Check the status on [Home](./home.md#stage) before going live.
