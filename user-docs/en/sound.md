## Noise reduction {#noise-reduction}

1. Open “Settings”, then “Sound”, and check “Noise reduction”.
2. Adjust with fan or air-conditioning noise present. Nearby speech and music are not fully removed.
3. [Listen in Voice Check](./sound.md#voice-check) for missing speech.

## Voice shaping {#voice-shaping}

1. Adjust “Shape voice” in “Sound”.
2. Check changes to bass, volume differences and tone one at a time.
3. Record and listen after each small change. If the voice becomes thin or sharp, reduce the adjustment and compare.

## VST {#vst}

1. Open the VST setup from Home and choose an effect to add.
2. Adjust values in each VST's editor.
3. Use “Compare without VST” to listen with and without external VST effects.

While the saved chain is protected, current VST changes are not saved automatically. Read the notice on screen.

## Singing and talking {#modes}

1. Save singing and talking adjustments in “Sound”.
2. Switch with “Sing” and “Talk” on Home.
3. If the mode also switches VSTs, sound may pause briefly. Check before going live.

“Custom sound” means no saved mode is in use. It does not mean current changes are saved.

## Voice Check {#voice-check}

1. Open “Voice Check” and check recording readiness.
2. Record your voice and compare before and after processing.
3. Check the full stream sound with an [OBS recording](./obs.md#verify) too.

Recording captures voice before output volume and global mute. Treat numerical assessments as references and judge by listening.
