## Current status {#stage}

Check “Running” or “Audio stopped”, the elapsed time, and the audio path on the band at the top of the center column. “Sing” and “Talk” switch there too. An unsaved adjustment is shown as “Custom sound”. “Choose destination” stays in Current status. There is at most one primary button. “Voice Check” opens from the right end of that band. While listening to music, the label is “Listen and compare”.

“Lock controls” is in the column on the right. While locked, it shows “Locked”. Stop and “Emergency mute” stay available.

## Meters {#meters}

In the right column, “Microphone” (“Playback source” while listening to music) shows incoming sound, and “Output” shows the level after processing. The bar follows the current level. The output number holds a recent peak. The output may also say “A bit low”, “In range”, or “Too loud”. Moving meters do not confirm that a stream or call is receiving you.

While waiting, silent, or stopped, the bars keep their size and position.

## Sound flow {#flow}

One band stacks the level before processing and the level after. The loudness check starts from the heading of this band.

## Sound adjustments {#sound}

Turn noise reduction and voice shaping on or off with the switch on each card. Strength and voice type stay on the card, without opening it. You can choose the strength, type, and amount while off. Choosing does not turn it on, and the sound does not change. On a tall window, the second row shows “Measure surrounding sound”, the voice type, and controls that open each VST. On a short window, open the same controls from “Basics” on the Sound page and from the VST page. Open the “VST” page from the rail. When none are loaded, use “Add”. When the count is unknown, or at least one is loaded, use “Open VST”. Change one thing at a time and [listen](./sound.md#voice-check).

## Audio check {#audio-check}

Use “Preparation” to check the connection and destination. On a wide window, preparation opens in a column on the right of Home, with “Connection details” expanded. On a narrow window, open it from “Connection details” below. Tell the output device apart from the destination, and confirm the receiving app as well.

## Live controls on the right {#output-volume}

The column on the right runs, from the top, through “Emergency mute”, microphone volume, “Output” and “Output volume”, “Compare without VST”, and “Lock controls”. Use “Output volume” to set the level. “Compare without VST” temporarily bypasses external VST effects only. Noise reduction and voice shaping continue.

To silence the sound immediately, use “Emergency mute” in the same column.
