## Live strip {#stage}

Read the status and mode name. Use “Choose destination” to select your purpose, such as streaming or calls. “Sing” and “Talk” switch saved adjustments. When no saved mode is in use, the display reads “Custom sound”.

“Lock controls” protects against accidental setting changes. Stop and emergency mute remain available.

## Meters {#meters}

“Voice” shows incoming sound; “Output” shows sound after processing. Moving meters do not confirm reception in a stream or call.

## Sound adjustments {#sound}

Open noise reduction, voice shaping or VST adjustments. Change one thing at a time and [listen](./sound.md#voice-check).

## Audio check {#audio-check}

Use “Preparation” to check connections and destination. “Connection details” leads to setup. Distinguish the output device from the destination purpose and check the receiving app too.

## Footer and output volume {#output-volume}

Adjust loudness with “Output volume”. “Compare without VST” temporarily bypasses external VST effects only. Noise reduction and voice shaping continue.

To silence audio immediately, use “Emergency mute” in the header.
