# Visual calibration

Use when a broad refinement request leaves the intervention unclear. A clear local correction can proceed directly.

## Identify the difference

Recover the requested outcome, current identity, and protected content or behavior. Translate “more professional”, “enterprise”, or “more impactful” into a specific issue in this context. Ask when different interpretations would materially change identity or scope.

Locate the relationship that needs work:

| Need | Possible intervention |
|---|---|
| Important content has too little presence | Strengthen its scale, position, contrast, imagery, or relationship to the action |
| Several elements compete | Redistribute emphasis, grouping, and rhythm |
| The interface feels inconsistent or unfinished | Resolve type roles, alignment, treatments, states, and continuity |

Change the resources involved in that issue. Reducing visual competition does not require removing expression, and increasing presence does not require adding effects everywhere.

## Choose the intervention

A vivid event page's signup action can gain presence through its position and relationship to the ticket details, while the large image and color remain. A visual collection may need a different proportion between work and metadata to make its artifacts lead. A working queue may need quieter summary emphasis so records lead, while useful totals and density stay.

These address similar attention problems through different choices. Compare the affected relationship and preserved content, rather than treating a larger button, new font, or neutral palette as a preset improvement.

Keep wording protected in visual-only work. Report material copy issues separately; copy-only work preserves presentation.

## Compare

Use matching content, states, and widths when before/after evidence is available. Check the intended improvement and recognizable character retained. Verify affected behavior and access independently. Correct observed gaps and recheck them; report rendering-dependent expectations as unverified when tools are unavailable.
